Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how clovical (clovical.in) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help websites function properly and remember information about your visit.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential cookies (always active)

  • Login session cookies — keep you signed in after logging in via Google, and let you securely access your cart and orders
  • Cart cookies — remember items in your cart as you browse

Third-party cookies

  • Google — sets cookies as part of the Google Sign-In login process
  • Cashfree — sets cookies during the payment checkout process to securely process your payment
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — used for bot/spam protection at checkout, sets a verification cookie

We do not currently use advertising or marketing tracking cookies.

3. Why We Use Cookies

Cookies help us:

  • Keep you logged in securely
  • Remember your cart between pages
  • Protect checkout from bots and fraud
  • Process payments securely through Cashfree

4. Managing Cookies

Most browsers let you control or delete cookies through their settings. Note that blocking essential cookies (like login/session cookies) may prevent you from logging in or completing a checkout.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date.

6. Contact Us

For any questions about this policy, contact us at eclovical@gmail.com.
